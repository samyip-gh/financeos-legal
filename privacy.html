Finance OS — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

Finance OS is a privately operated, non-commercial personal finance application. It is intended for the account holder's own use and is not a public financial service.

Information accessed

When the account holder explicitly authorizes a bank connection, Finance OS is designed to retrieve account information made available through Enable Banking, such as account identifiers, balances and transaction records. Access is subject to the permissions granted by the account holder and the bank.

Purpose and payment access

Account information is used for personal financial tracking, reconciliation and analysis. Finance OS is designed for account-information access only; it does not provide a payment-initiation feature.

Storage and disclosure

The application is designed to store sensitive banking credentials and retrieved financial records in user-controlled, encrypted local storage. Banking records, private signing keys and access tokens are not published on this website or in its public GitHub repository. Data necessarily passes through the account holder's bank and Enable Banking to provide the authorized connection; those parties operate under their own privacy notices and terms.

Retention and control

The account holder controls the local Finance OS data and can delete locally stored records. Bank authorization can be withdrawn using the available bank or Enable Banking consent-management process. Some information may remain with the bank or Enable Banking under their respective retention policies.

This website

These informational pages do not ask visitors to enter financial data and do not contain application analytics or advertising scripts. They are hosted by GitHub Pages, which may process technical access information under GitHub's own privacy practices.

Contact

For questions about these public documents, visit the Finance OS legal repository. Do not post bank account details, credentials or other sensitive personal information in public GitHub issues.
