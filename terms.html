Finance OS — Terms of Use

Last updated: 22 October 2026

Personal use only

Finance OS is a privately operated personal finance tool intended solely for its account holder. It is not offered as a commercial service to the public, and these pages do not invite third parties to register or connect bank accounts.

Authorized account information

Finance OS is intended to retrieve account balances and transactions only after the account holder grants the required authorization through the bank and Enable Banking. It is not intended to initiate payments or transfer funds.

No financial advice

Finance OS organizes financial information for personal reference. It does not provide regulated investment, legal, tax or financial advice. Bank statements and official records remain authoritative.

Availability and accuracy

Data access depends on the account holder's bank, Enable Banking, network availability and valid authorization. Information may be delayed, incomplete or unavailable, and the application may be changed or discontinued without notice.

Third-party services

Bank connectivity is subject to the bank's and Enable Banking's applicable terms, consent requirements and privacy practices. Public informational pages are hosted by GitHub Pages.

Privacy

See the Finance OS Privacy Policy for information about account data and local storage.

Contact

Questions about these public documents can be directed through the Finance OS legal repository. Do not disclose sensitive banking information in public GitHub discussions.
